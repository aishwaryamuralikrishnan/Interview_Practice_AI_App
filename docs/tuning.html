What you can actually tune

Two dials, and one that does not exist on this model family. Both dials trade quality against cost and speed — neither of them controls how varied the wording is.

Dial 1 Reasoning effort

How long the model thinks before it answers. The thinking is invisible — you never see it — but you pay for it, and deeper thinking gives sharper questions and better calibrated scores. This is the nearest thing to a temperature dial, and it changes depth, not randomness.

Dial 2 Max tokens per response

A ceiling, not a budget you spend — you are billed for what is generated, so a generous cap costs nothing. The catch on a reasoning model: the cap covers the invisible thinking as well as the visible answer.

Not available Temperature

No GPT-5 model accepts temperature, top_p or verbosity — they are not among the parameters the provider lists, and sending one is rejected outright.

So the variety of the wording cannot be turned up or down. Reasoning effort replaces it as the quality dial, but it is not the same thing: it changes how hard the model thinks, not how adventurous it is.
