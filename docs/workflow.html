How the app works, end to end

Five stages. You choose the model and the interview's shape; the app reads the posting, conducts the interview one question at a time, and scores every answer against a fixed rubric at the end.

& model

You

The app

no model call
4

Mock
interview

You

Answer one question at a time, the way you would out loud.

The app

Asks the next question only after you have answered, with the whole conversation so far in view.

1 call per question
5

Report
& PDF

The app

Scores each answer against its rubric, writes a stronger version of it, and draws the charts. Downloadable as PDF.

1 call per answer + 1 summary
↻ repeats until every question is answered —
the next question is never written in advance
Why one question at a time. Each new question is generated with the full transcript in context, so a follow-up can build on what you actually said — which a pre-written list of questions cannot do.
Why scoring waits until the end. Feedback mid-interview would let you correct course, which a real interview never allows. The rubric is applied once, to the answers as given.
